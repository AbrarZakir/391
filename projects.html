AutomateClass

AutomateClass is a Voice-Agentic LMS for coaching centers in Bangladesh, covering all curricula. It handles batches, a feed, a Teaching Assistant portal, and an SMS center. It is built on Next.js, Supabase, and Cloudflare R2.

AutomatePapers

AutomatePapers is a one-stop solution for English medium resources: a Cambridge and Edexcel past paper platform with strong organic traffic and users in Bangladesh, the UK, Spain, Egypt, the USA, and Dubai. You can open the question paper and mark scheme side by side, and it also has examiner reports, solved papers, timed mocks, and textbook PDFs. It also has a Teacher-mode question builder and printed chapterwise books.
